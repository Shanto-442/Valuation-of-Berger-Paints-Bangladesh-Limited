## 🧠 Valuation Methodology
We utilized a multi-pronged approach to determine the intrinsic value of BPBL:

### 1. Fundamental Analysis
* **Macroeconomic Review:** Analyzed GDP growth, urbanization rates, and raw material price trends affecting the paint sector.
* **Industry Analysis:** Applied **Porter’s 5 Forces** to evaluate competitive intensity and barriers to entry.

### 2. Financial Modeling
* **Forecasting:** Projected revenues and costs for a 5-year explicit period based on historical regression and market outlook.
* **DCF Analysis:** Calculated the **Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)** and discounted it using a calculated Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).
* **Relative Valuation:** Benchmarked BPBL against regional peers (e.g., Asian Paints, Berger Pakistan) using **P/E** and **EV/EBITDA** multiples.

### 3. Risk Assessment
* Conducted **Sensitivity Analysis** to stress-test the target price against fluctuations in Foreign Exchange (FX) rates and key raw material costs.
